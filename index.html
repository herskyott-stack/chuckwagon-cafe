The Famous Bennys

Our all-day breakfast is built around the “Famous Benny’s” — choose the Original Eggs Benedict, Smoked Salmon Benedict, Flat Iron Steak Benedict or the Triple A Tenderloin Steak Benedict, all served on a fresh croissant. As seen on You Gotta Eat Here!

Ranch Beef, Done Right

Over half the beef we serve comes from owner Terry Myhre’s own ranch and is dry-aged in nearby High River. That’s the beef in the House Burger — voted “#1 Burger in Alberta” by Avenue Magazine and the Calgary Herald three years running — and in our beef barley soup, made from a rich, dark stock of roasted ox tails.

Homemade Pies

The pie case changes with the season: banana cream, rhubarb sour cream, chocolate silk, pumpkin, and the famous flapper — the lost prairie pie. Worth the drive on its own.
